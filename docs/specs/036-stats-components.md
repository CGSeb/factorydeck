# 036 · Stats components

Status: ready
Depends on: 018, 033, 035
Checked: 2026-11-22

## User goal

A developer will read on a statistics page whether their factory is doing well and getting better: key figures with their change against the previous period and the number of items behind them, trends week by week, the spread of serenity scores, why a person had to step in, and how lead times and costs spread between issues. This spec builds those components in Storybook, ready for the panels of specs 037 to 039; no page changes yet.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `src/ui/components/stats-math.ts` exports `MIN_SAMPLE = 5`, `median(values)`, `percentile(values, p)`, `changeText(...)` and the histogram binning below, as pure functions. Every component of this spec takes its sample size `n` from the caller and, when `n` is below `MIN_SAMPLE`, says `Not enough data` instead of a figure, as each criterion below states.
- [ ] `src/ui/components/StatFigure.tsx` takes a label, a value (a number or `null`), a `format` function for it, a kind (`share`, a fraction from 0 to 1, or `amount`, any count, duration or token figure), a sample size `n` with its noun (singular and plural, such as `merged issue` / `merged issues`), and optionally a previous value, its own sample size, the comparison text (such as `previous 30 days`), which direction is better (`higher` or `lower`) and a note (a short text such as `2 still waiting`). It shows the label (13 px, `--fd-muted`), the formatted value (`--fd-title-md`, `--fd-font-display`, weight 600, tabular figures), then `n = 14 merged issues` (`n = 1 merged issue`; 12 px, `--fd-muted`), then the change line, then the note when given (12 px, `--fd-ink-2`), shown whatever the sample size.
- [ ] With `n` below `MIN_SAMPLE` or a `null` value, `StatFigure` shows `—` as its value and `Not enough data` in place of the `n = …` line, and no change line.
- [ ] The change line reads `▲ <amount> vs <comparison>` or `▼ <amount> vs <comparison>`: for a `share`, the difference in whole percentage points (`▲ 12 pts`, `▼ 1 pt`); for an `amount`, the relative difference in whole percent of the previous value (`▼ 18%`). The arrow is `aria-hidden` and a visually hidden `up` or `down` takes its place for screen readers. A change that rounds to 0 reads `No change vs <comparison>` in `--fd-muted`. Otherwise the line is in `--fd-success` when the change goes the better way and `--fd-danger` when it does not; the text alone tells the direction. There is no change line when no previous value is given, when the previous value is `null`, when the previous sample is below `MIN_SAMPLE`, or, for an `amount`, when the previous value is 0.
- [ ] `src/ui/components/TrendBars.tsx` takes buckets (`label`, `value` or `null`, an optional `n`, and the value's text for the tooltip), a scale (`share`: a bar's height is its value out of 100%; `count`: out of the largest value of the chart), a colour and a height in px. It draws the columns like `DayBars` of spec 018 (bottom-aligned, gap 6 px, radius 4 px on top, a 2 px stub at 0, first and last labels at the two ends, every label with 7 buckets or fewer). A bucket that has an `n` below `MIN_SAMPLE`, or whose value is `null`, draws a 2 px stub in `--fd-line`, whatever its value. A bucket without `n` (a plain count, such as issues merged that week) always shows its value. Hovering a column shows a tooltip in the chart tooltip style with the label, the value's text and, when the bucket has an `n`, `n = 7`; or the label and `Not enough data`; the other columns dim. With the `count` scale, the largest value is taken among the buckets drawn with a value.
- [ ] `TrendBars` has `role="img"`, an accessible name given by the caller, and a visually hidden table of every bucket with its value's text (or `Not enough data`) and its `n`.
- [ ] `src/ui/components/ScoreHistogram.tsx` takes serenity reports, each with its score (a whole number from 0 to 100), its own threshold and its time, since the threshold of a project can change between reports (spec 034). It groups the scores into 20 bins of 5 points (`0–4`, `5–9`, …, `90–94`, `95–100`) and draws one column per bin, height proportional to the fullest bin, a bin with no report drawn as a 2 px stub in `--fd-line`. Each column stacks, from the bottom, the reports whose score is below their own threshold, filled `--fd-warning`, then those at or above it, filled `--fd-success`, with no gap between the two parts. A vertical line in `--fd-ink-2` marks the threshold of the latest report on the 0 to 100 axis, labelled `threshold 80` above the chart, or `threshold 80 · varied` when at least one report has another threshold. Under the chart, `0`, `50` and `100` sit at their positions. Hovering a column shows `85–89` over `4 reports` (`1 report`), followed by ` · 1 below its threshold` (` · 2 below their threshold`) when some are, and dims the others.
- [ ] With fewer reports than `MIN_SAMPLE`, `ScoreHistogram` draws no column and shows `Not enough data` in the chart's place, at the same height.
- [ ] `ScoreHistogram` has `role="img"`, an accessible name given by the caller, and a visually hidden table of every bin that holds at least one report, with its count and how many are below their threshold, followed by the latest threshold (and `varied` when thresholds differ).
- [ ] `src/ui/components/CountBars.tsx` takes rows (`label`, `count`), a total (the number of items the rows count among) and the items' noun. It shows a list, one row per item: the label, a 10 px track (`--fd-line`, pill) with a bar in `--fd-warning` whose width is `count / total` (at most the whole track), then `<count> · <share>%` (`5 · 42%`, whole percent, `<1%` for a share above 0 that rounds to 0; tabular figures, right-aligned). Rows are sorted by count descending, ties in the given order; rows at 0 stay, with no bar. Under the list, `among <total> <noun>` (`among 12 merged issues`, `among 1 merged issue`) in 12 px `--fd-muted`. The rows may add up to more than the total: an item can count in several rows.
- [ ] With a total below `MIN_SAMPLE`, `CountBars` shows the labels with no bar and `Not enough data` in place of every `<count> · <share>%`.
- [ ] `CountBars` is a list for screen readers, each item reading its label, count and share; the tracks are `aria-hidden`.
- [ ] `src/ui/components/RangeStrip.tsx` takes items (`label`, `value`, `href`), a `format` function for values and an accessible name. It draws a horizontal 2 px line in `--fd-border` from 0 to the largest value (linear), with `0` under its left end and the largest value, formatted, under its right end. Each item is an 8 px dot in `--fd-ink-2` at its value; a dot whose centre is less than 8 px from a dot already placed (items taken in value order) stacks 10 px above it, so no dot hides another. A stack holds at most 5 levels, so the strip's height is fixed: a stack of `m` items with `m` above 5 draws its 4 lowest dots and, on the 5th level, a pill `+<m − 4>` (12 px, `--fd-ink-2` on `--fd-surface-2`). With the largest value at 0, every dot sits at the left end.
- [ ] Each `RangeStrip` dot is a link to its `href`, in value order for the keyboard, with the accessible name `<label>, <formatted value>`. Hovering or focusing a dot shows a tooltip in the chart tooltip style with the label over the formatted value (`#42 Fold status groups` / `3.1M tokens`) and an accent ring around the dot. An item folded into a `+k` pill keeps its link, in the same value order, visually hidden: when it has focus, the pill shows the accent ring and the item's tooltip.
- [ ] Above the line, `RangeStrip` marks the median and the 90th percentile with a 1 px vertical tick in `--fd-ink` and a label (`median 1.2M`, `p90 4.8M`, 12 px, values formatted by `format`); when the two labels would overlap, the `p90` label moves to a second line. With fewer items than `MIN_SAMPLE`, the dots show but neither mark does, and `Not enough data for median and p90` sits under the strip in 12 px `--fd-muted`. With no item, the strip shows `No data` in place of the line.
- [ ] No component of this spec makes the page scroll horizontally at 360 px: each sizes to its container.
- [ ] Storybook stories, each in the light and dark theme through the `Theme` toolbar of spec 033: `StatFigure` (share going the better way, amount going the worse way, `lower` is better and falling, no change, no previous value, previous value 0, below the minimum sample, `null` value, with a note); `TrendBars` (12 weeks of shares, 12 weeks of counts, some weeks below the minimum sample, all zero, hovered column); `ScoreHistogram` (scores around a threshold of 80, a threshold of 72 inside a bin so one column holds both tones, thresholds that changed from 75 to 80, every score above, below the minimum sample, hovered column); `CountBars` (five reasons with overlap, a reason at 0, a tie, below the minimum sample); `RangeStrip` (30 issues with one outlier, many equal values stacking into a `+k` pill (a `play` that tabs to a folded item and checks its tooltip), close median and p90 labels, 3 items, no item, a `play` that tabs to a dot and checks its tooltip). They pass the axe check of `src/ui/stories.test.tsx`.

## Rules

- **Median**: the middle value of the sorted values, or the mean of the two middle values when their number is even. **Percentile** (`p90`): nearest rank, the value at position `ceil(p × n)` (from 1) of the sorted values. Both return `null` for no value.
- **Change**: compared with the unrounded values; for a `share`, `round((value − previous) × 100)` points; for an `amount`, `round((value − previous) / previous × 100)` percent. A negative zero is `No change`.
- **Score bins**: a score of 100 falls in `95–100`; scores outside 0 to 100 are clamped to it. A report is below its threshold when its score is strictly lower than its own threshold, as `below-threshold` in `refusalReasons` of `src/git-host/outcome.ts` (spec 035). The latest report is the one with the latest time; ties keep the last given.
- Below `MIN_SAMPLE`, a component never shows a figure computed on that sample, whatever the scale; `Not enough data` is the only wording.
- The caller formats values, gives labels, nouns and comparison texts; the components hold no workflow vocabulary (no role, stage or label name).
- Charts are HTML and CSS only, colours from the tokens of spec 014 and 033, figures in tabular numbers, as spec 018.

## Expected proof

- Vitest tests of `stats-math.ts`: median (odd, even, one value, none), `percentile` (nearest rank at 90 on 1, 10 and 11 values, none), change text and rounding (share points, amount percent, rounding to 0, negative zero, previous 0), score binning (0, 4, 5, 95, 100, out of range), the split of a bin by each report's own threshold and the `varied` flag, and the dot stacking of `RangeStrip` (equal values, values 7 px and 9 px apart, a stack of 5 and of 8 items folding into `+4`).
- The stories above pass the axe check of `src/ui/stories.test.tsx`.
- In Storybook (`pnpm storybook`): screenshots of each component's main story in the light and dark theme, attached to the pull request.
